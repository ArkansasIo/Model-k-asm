# Model K QISA v1

Allocation: QALLOC, QFREE, RESET.

Single-qubit gates: X, Y, Z, H, S, SDG, T, TDG, RX, RY, RZ.

Two-qubit gates: CX, CZ, SWAP.

Classical boundary: MEASURE, MOV, CMP, JMP, JZ, JNZ, HALT.

QISA is an intermediate representation and does not claim that magnetic-core hardware directly implements quantum gates.
